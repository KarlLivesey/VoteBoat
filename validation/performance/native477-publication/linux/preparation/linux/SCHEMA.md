## Transitions, failures and cleanup

Controller states are Prepared -> Guarded -> Timing -> Terminal -> Collected.
Do not enter Timing without actual published-source/native fresh-library proof,
successful selected release controls, frozen imported validator closure and
matching original/copy executable hashes. Record the original live job handle
and durable status/terminal markers; an observation timeout does not restart it.
Child failure stays in place with its raw files and actual exit. Continue only
according to the frozen controller policy, never replace a failed case or retune
workloads/deadlines. Collection begins after actual termination. Successful
per-case store cleanup follows the existing fixture; failed or uncertain stores
remain for diagnosis. Archive raw text/CSV/JSON and lossless large outputs with
original hashes and lengths. Do not transfer build trees, binaries or generated
WALs between hosts or relabel old qualification receipts.
